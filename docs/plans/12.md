## Goal
Customer-facing incidents receive the one-level priority increase at any hour, while routing behavior remains unchanged.

## Steps
1. Update `incidentdesk/priority.py` so customer-facing priority adjustments do not depend on business hours.
2. Update `tests/unit/test_priority.py` with one test for each example in the priority-rules table, including the nighttime and weekend cases.
3. Add a plain-language entry under Unreleased in `CHANGELOG.md`.

## Tests
- Tuesday 14:00, major `login-service`: assert P1 in `tests/unit/test_priority.py`.
- Tuesday 23:30, major `login-service`: assert P1 in `tests/unit/test_priority.py`.
- Saturday 10:00, major `login-service`: assert P1 in `tests/unit/test_priority.py`.
- Run the priority and routing tests; routing tests should pass unchanged.

## Risks
Changing the priority calculation could accidentally affect non-customer-facing or maintenance-window incidents; preserve their existing behavior and verify them with the current priority tests. Routing could change unintentionally; verify its tests pass without edits.

## Out of scope
Previously stored incidents, routing behavior, and `is_business_hours`.
